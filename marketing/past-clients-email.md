# Past-Client Reactivation — 2-Email Sequence

**Audience:** Previous Milktree project clients — the 200+ brands built over
six years. They already trust the work; the only news is the model.
This is the warmest list you own, so it gets the founding rate FIRST,
framed as a former-client privilege rather than a public promo.

**Sender:** the founder, personally. Plain text. Send in small batches so
replies are manageable, and personalise the project reference by hand —
these people bought from you, they'll notice a mail merge.

**Cadence:** Email 1, then email 2 seven days later to non-responders only.

**Tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{project}}` (what we built for
them, e.g. "the EazyPhone identity", "your packaging"), `{{year}}`.

---

## Email 1 — The announcement (personal)

**Subject:** we changed how Milktree works

> {{first_name}} — it's been a while since we wrapped {{project}}. Hope it's
> still working hard for {{company}}.
>
> Writing because we've changed how Milktree works, and former clients should
> hear it from me before anyone else.
>
> We've stopped doing project quotes. Milktree now works as an embedded design
> team on a flat monthly subscription: unlimited requests, senior work back in
> around 48 hours, a creative director on every piece. Brand, ads, decks,
> packaging, web — everything we did for you, but on tap instead of per
> proposal.
>
> Two plans:
>
> - **Essentials — £1,999/mo.** Your design queue handled, one request at a
>   time, pause anytime.
> - **Design Lead — £3,999/mo.** Two at a time, plus your own dedicated senior
>   designer — the same person every time — direct on your Slack.
>
> Here's the part that's for you: the first 10 Design Lead clients lock
> **£3,500/mo for life**, and I'm offering those spots to former clients before
> we open them up fully. No contract either way — cancel any month.
>
> Since we already know {{company}}'s brand, there's no onboarding. You could
> have work in the queue this week.
>
> Interested? Just reply, or grab a call here: [Cal.com link]
>
> — [Founder name]

## Segmentation notes

- **Segment A — clients from the last 2 years:** send as-is. Highest intent.
- **Segment B — older clients (3–6 years):** same emails, but swap the
  {{project}} opener for "you were one of the first brands we built" framing —
  nostalgia plus "look how far the work has come" (link one recent case study).
- **Skip:** anyone with an unresolved dispute or bad ending. One bad forward
  poisons a niche.
- Replies asking for project work, not subscription: the answer is
  "we don't do one-off projects any more, but a single month of Essentials
  covers what you're describing, and you can cancel right after." That
  one-month framing converts fence-sitters.
- Track spot count manually — if founding spots fill from ads or the site,
  update or pull email 2 before the next batch goes out.
