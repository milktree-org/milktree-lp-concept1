# Meta Ads — 6 Static Creatives

All statics. Black canvas, huge Satoshi type, one yellow element per creative, max.
Build each in 1:1 and 4:5. Destination for every ad: `milktreeagency.com/start`.
CTA button: **Get Started** on all ads.

Voice rules: no hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji. First line of primary text
lands before Meta's "...See more" truncation (~125 chars).

Test structure: Ads 1–5 in one prospecting ad set. Ad 6 in a separate
retargeting ad set only (scarcity works on people who already know the offer).

---

## Ad 1 — Pain hook (cold prospecting)

**On-image hook:**
> Your freelancer just ghosted. Again.

**Support line:**
> Get an embedded design team instead. Senior work back in ~48 hours.

**Yellow element:** the word "Again." or the CTA chip "Get started →"

**Alt hooks (separate statics):**
- "Still briefing five freelancers to do one job?"
- "Your marketing team has the ideas. Not the firepower to ship them."

**Primary text:**

Freelancers ghost when the deadline actually matters. Hiring takes months and £50k+ for one skill set. Agencies send a proposal for every small thing.

There's a fourth option.

Milktree is your embedded design team on subscription. Unlimited requests, senior work back in ~48 hours, one flat monthly fee. Brand, ads, decks, packaging, web — from the team behind 200+ brands.

No contracts. Pause or cancel any month.

**Headline:** Your creative department. On demand.
**Description:** Senior design, ~48h turnaround. No contracts.

## Ad 5 — Mechanism hook (objection-removal, warm traffic)

**On-image hook:**
> No proposals. No quotes. No contracts.

**Support line (tiny, stacked):**
> Subscribe → Request → Receive in ~48h → Approve

**Yellow element:** the arrow/line connecting the steps.

**Alt hook (strong — consider its own creative):**
> Brief on Monday. Approved by Wednesday.

**Primary text:**

How it works:

1. Subscribe. Dashboard access the same day.
2. Request. Unlimited briefs in your queue: brand, ads, decks, social, packaging, web.
3. Receive. Senior work back in around 48 hours, creative-directed so nothing ships off-brand.
4. Approve, or send it back. One revision round included on every request.

Pause whenever things go quiet and unused time banks. That's the whole model.

**Headline:** Subscribe. Request. Receive in ~48h.
**Description:** No proposals, no quotes, no contracts.

## Production notes

- Hook must read at thumbnail size: 1–2 lines, parse in under 2 seconds.
- Keep image text under ~20% of the dominant canvas area.
- 4:5 wins feed real estate; skip 9:16 text statics unless redesigned for Stories.
- No testimonial-based ads until there are named, attributed client quotes
  (current quotes in `lib/site.ts` are placeholders — ASA/Meta risk).
