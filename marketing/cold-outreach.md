# Cold Email Outreach — 4-Step Sequence

**Audience:** Founders, marketing leads and heads of growth at scaling UK
companies (roughly 10–100 headcount) who are visibly shipping marketing —
running ads, posting, hiring for marketing roles — but whose design is
inconsistent or clearly stretched.

**Sender:** a real person (founder or creative director), plain-text emails,
no HTML templates, no images, no links except the one CTA. Cold email that
looks like marketing gets deleted; cold email that looks like a note gets read.

**Cadence:** Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 9 → Day 16. Stop on any reply.

**Personalisation tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`,
`{{specific_observation}}` — the observation is mandatory on email 1.
One genuine sentence about something they shipped (an ad you saw, a rebrand,
a new product page). No observation, no send.

## Rules

- Stop the sequence on ANY reply, including "not now" — move those to a
  90-day follow-up list.
- Never send email 1 without a real `{{specific_observation}}`.
- UK PECR/GDPR: B2B cold email to corporate addresses is lawful under
  legitimate interest, but include the company's registered address and a
  working opt-out line in the footer, and honour opt-outs immediately.
- Keep the founding-rate spot count in email 4 vague ("a few spots left")
  so sequences already in flight don't go stale — the exact number lives
  on the site.
